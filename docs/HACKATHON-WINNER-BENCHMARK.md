# Make Bilad’s Verdict Decision Ready

Historical research reference. The user subsequently chose a global product;
[GLOBAL-DIRECTION.md](GLOBAL-DIRECTION.md) supersedes the nation-led scope and
homepage recommendations below. Keep the evidence and usability findings as
research, not as a description of the current implementation.

NASA Space Apps winners are impressive when a visitor can see one recognizable loop: a named user selects a place or dataset, the product transforms NASA data into a decision or learning output, and the product shows a compact proof of why that output deserves attention. Bilad already has a stronger scientific core than many public winner surfaces: it maps a nation, estimates trends, tests significance, checks an independent record where one exists, screens for step changes, and states its limitations. The gap is presentation and productization. The current experience makes a judge assemble the claim from a dense hero, map, controls, and long evidence panel; it does not yet make the next action, witness status, shareable evidence, or national brief obvious. Claude Code should therefore improve hierarchy, decision output, provenance, and release consistency while preserving Bilad’s national Earth-system atlas and its refusal to claim causation, forecasting, or precision the data cannot support.

## NASA winners turn complex data into a short, consequential loop

### Observed facts

Bilad targets the 2026 NASA challenge, which asks teams to select Earth-science variables, visualize how they change over time, and determine what is changing, where, by how much, and whether the change is statistically significant ([NASA challenge statement](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/earth-system-trend-detective/)). NASA’s award definitions explicitly reward valid science, unique or accessible use of space data, plausible design, potential impact, and storytelling ([NASA Space Apps awards](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/)). Local judging guidance groups the evaluation around Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, and Presentation and gives teams three minutes to pitch before judges score or ask questions ([NASA Space Apps judging guidance](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/local-events/isa-town/)). These are not instructions to add more features; they are pressure toward a legible claim, usable prototype, and credible explanation.

The NASA primary comparison set reviewed here is the 2023–2025 global winners. The official NASA announcements describe recurring product shapes:

- **Data to a decision.** Storm Prophet turns DSCOVR observations into geomagnetic-storm forecasts; NASA recognized it with the 2023 Best Use of Data award ([NASA 2023 winners](https://science.nasa.gov/directorates/smd/2023-nasa-international-space-apps-challenge-announces-10-global-winners/)). The team’s project page supplies the data window, model choice, demo, repository, and evaluation description; its accuracy figures are team-reported, not independently evaluated in the reviewed sources ([Storm Prophet project page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2023/find-a-team/storm-prophet/?tab=project)).
- **Place to action.** 2plant received the 2024 Galactic Impact award. Its official project page reduces the farmer’s problem to two questions—whether soil moisture is sufficient for germination and whether equipment can safely enter the field—then presents daily risk assessments, a planting-window timeline, uncertainty, and a “second opinion” view ([NASA 2024 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-international-space-apps-challenge-announces-2024-global-winners/); [2plant project page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/nasa-space-apps-2024/find-a-team/nvs-knot/?tab=project)).
- **Map to trigger to evidence.** Landsat Connect, the 2024 Best Mission Concept winner, lets a user set a target location, receive an alert before a Landsat pass, and then inspect the relevant surface-reflectance data. The team-owned site adds metadata, a 3×3 pixel context, spectral information, and CSV/PDF export ([NASA 2024 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-international-space-apps-challenge-announces-2024-global-winners/); [Landsat Connect](https://www.asturexplorers.space/landsat-connect-app/)). The site describes several capabilities in future tense, so those details are product intent or prototype scope, not proof of sustained deployment.
- **Dataset to formula to inspectable output.** SpaceGenes+ won Best Use of Science in 2025. Its public product page names the audience, shows a three-step input/analysis/output flow, prints its synergy formula, links results to a NASA GeneLab study, offers an exportable heatmap, and explicitly says only one case is live while the others are conceptual ([NASA 2025 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/); [SpaceGenes+](https://spacegenes-plus.framer.website/)).
- **Analysis to a safe window.** NASA describes SkySense, the 2025 Best Use of Technology winner, as combining Earth-observation data and AI analysis into ultra-local weather predictions, risk assessments, and the safest time windows for activities ([NASA 2025 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/)).
- **Local problem to local intervention.** QUEÑARIS won the 2025 Local Impact award for a project framed around Arequipa’s water scarcity and degraded queñua forests. NASA’s description connects satellite data, drones, native microorganisms, and AI to reforestation-site selection and ecosystem monitoring ([NASA 2025 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/)).

### Interpretation for Bilad

The transferable pattern is not “use AI,” “make a globe,” or “add a dashboard.” It is:

**named user or stake → one bounded question → one visible result → one proof artifact → one next action.**

For Bilad, the bounded question is already present: “Did this national trend survive significance testing, an integrity screen, and an independent record?” The missing product object is the visible result that closes the loop. A map layer should terminate in a sentence, verdict, watchlist, or brief—not leave the reader to infer what the selected colors mean. A national atlas can remain the core; the atlas needs a briefing mode that makes its output reusable.

The winners also make prototype boundaries explicit. SpaceGenes+ says which case is live. Landsat Connect’s future-tense language signals planned scope. Storm Prophet exposes team-reported evaluation rather than presenting it as independent certification. Bilad should use the same epistemic discipline: label shipped data, computed evidence, team interpretation, and future work separately. Awards establish recognition by an organizer; they do not prove adoption, uptime, peer review, or independent scientific validation.

## Bilad already has the scientific moat; the problem is underexposure

### Observed facts in the current repository

Bilad already presents a national Earth-system atlas rather than a generic dashboard. The README defines a 44-year, seven-variable NASA POWER/MERRA-2 record, national maps, four case files, a shareable Atlas URL state, a Method page, and an offline static deployment with no key or backend ([README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:3), [README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:24), [README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:39)). The four cases intentionally end in different outcomes: corroborated, unwitnessed at the witness resolution, contradicted/likely artefact, and same direction with a different size ([src/data/cases.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/data/cases.ts:32)).

The scientific chain is unusually inspectable for a hackathon prototype. Bilad uses MERRA-2 on its native grid, monthly deseasonalisation, land-area weighting, Theil–Sen slopes, Hamed–Rao-corrected Mann–Kendall tests, Benjamini–Hochberg field control, a ramp-versus-jump BIC screen, and GISTEMP/GPCP cross-examination ([README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:39)). The browser and offline summary pipeline share the TypeScript statistics engine, while Python independently recomputes shipped headlines ([README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:52), [scripts/summarize.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/scripts/summarize.ts:1), [tests/data.test.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/tests/data.test.ts:30)). Provenance is in the data model and displayed through NASA POWER request URLs ([src/lib/nationData.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/lib/nationData.ts:10), [src/features/EvidencePanel.tsx](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/features/EvidencePanel.tsx:247)).

Bilad also does the most important scientific-honesty work correctly. The Method page states that a detected trend does not establish why it changed and that Bilad does not attribute causes, forecast, or downscale below the reanalysis grid ([src/pages/Method.tsx](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/pages/Method.tsx:158)). The README names coarse witnesses, shared rain-gauge inputs, ocean influence in coastal cells, and spatial non-independence as limitations ([README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:104)). This is a strength to preserve, not copy-edit away.

### What Bilad has that strong winners avoid

The current product makes a visitor do too much interpretation before reaching the product’s key conclusion. The home hero introduces Chile, Bangladesh, MERRA-2, an independent witness, and significance testing while the primary visual is a large animated map plus a 44-row year ledger ([src/pages/Casebook.tsx](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/pages/Casebook.tsx:94)). The Atlas exposes four layers, a significance switch, a two-ended analysis window, region and latitude-band focus, cell selection, and a long evidence panel. None of these controls is inherently wrong; the issue is that the first-time path does not stage them around one decision.

Bilad also has a “raw evidence without a reusable output” gap. The EvidencePanel already includes the headline, chart, cross-examination, integrity screen, month-by-month trends, start-year sensitivity, full test record, field counts, and provenance ([src/features/EvidencePanel.tsx](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/features/EvidencePanel.tsx:92)). But the router has no brief/report route, export route, or explicit copy/share action beyond the technically shareable hash URL ([src/app/router.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/app/router.ts:1), [src/app/router.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/app/router.ts:29)). Strong public winners make their result portable through an alert, report, export, dated page, or reproducible notebook.

The current witness model is scientifically honest but not yet operationally clear. Only T2M and PRECTOTCORR are witnessed ([src/lib/evidence.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/lib/evidence.ts:41)). In the detail panel, a missing examination becomes “No independent record is wired in,” while a failed request can arrive through the same null path and read like an intentional absence ([src/features/EvidencePanel.tsx](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/features/EvidencePanel.tsx:161), [src/lib/hooks.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/lib/hooks.ts:16)). The product needs distinct states for unsupported, unavailable, and failed/retryable; otherwise the strongest trust feature becomes ambiguous.

Release consistency is a direct trust risk. The source registry and generated public/data/index.json currently contain seven nations—BGD, CHL, KEN, MEX, MAR, PHL, and ESP—while the README says six, tests/data.test.ts expects six, and submission/casebook copy still contains six-nation language ([pipeline/nations.py](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/pipeline/nations.py:42), [public/data/index.json](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/public/data/index.json), [README.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/README.md:24), [tests/data.test.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/tests/data.test.ts:24), [docs/SUBMISSION.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/docs/SUBMISSION.md:11)). The current Casebook derives some counts from the index, which makes the mismatch easy for a judge to see. The checked-in demo PDF is also documented as showing a previous version ([docs/SUBMISSION.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/docs/SUBMISSION.md:3)).

Finally, the code contains analysis or visualization scaffolding that is not part of the visible product: breakYear is computed, but there is no change-point layer; loadWorld, yearRamp, and BIVARIATE remain after the globe/compound modes were cut ([src/lib/analysis.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/lib/analysis.ts:34), [src/lib/nationData.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/lib/nationData.ts:75), [src/viz/color.ts](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/src/viz/color.ts:90), [tasks/plan.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/tasks/plan.md:81)). This is not a runtime defect by itself, but it increases the distance between code, UI, and the story Claude Code is being asked to improve.

### What is missing or underexposed

The missing pieces are product surfaces, not new science:

- A **one-screen verdict strip**: what changed, where, over which window, by how much per decade, whether it survives the significance screen, whether a witness exists and agrees, and what to inspect next.
- A **task-shaped start**: “Start with the four cases,” “inspect a nation,” or “open a national brief,” rather than presenting a blank conceptual space of variables and layers.
- **Progressive disclosure**: summary, chart, verdict, and next action open first; seasonal detail, sensitivity, raw test record, co-break detail, and URLs available under labeled audit sections.
- A **national evidence brief** and **downloadable receipt** for all seven variables, with claims, intervals, p-values, field counts, witness coverage, source date, resolution, baseline, and limitations.
- **Visible share/export**: the existing URL-state design should become a user-facing capability.
- **Consistent release proof**: counts, copy, tests, generated data, demo artifact, source links, and browser behavior must describe the same build.
- **Browser acceptance evidence**: the written specification calls for desktop/mobile behavior, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, network failure, and source-link checks, but the current CI workflow only runs unit/data tests, independent recomputation, lint, build, and bundle-size checks ([SPEC.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/SPEC.md:129), [.github/workflows/deploy.yml](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/.github/workflows/deploy.yml:40)).

## Implementation brief: package the existing atlas before expanding it

Claude Code should preserve the current national Earth-system model, data pipeline, statistics engine, independent witnesses, four verdict cases, and limitations language. The goal is a briefing layer over the existing Atlas, not a new global product.

### Guardrails: what to copy and what not to copy

Copy the interaction topology from winners: named question, visible result, compact proof, reusable output, honest status. Do not copy their branding, logo language, color identity, domain logic, market positioning, or NASA-adjacent visual cues. NASA’s legal terms require source citation and open availability for submitted original content; NASA’s brand guide is a presentation reference, not permission to imply NASA endorsement ([NASA Space Apps legal terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/); [NASA Space Apps brand guide](https://assets.spaceappschallenge.org/media/documents/Space_Apps_Brand_Guide_-_23_May_2025.pdf)).

Do not add a chatbot merely because some broader winners use conversational interfaces. Bilad’s differentiator is transparent trend evidence and cross-examination. A conversational layer would be justified only if it answered a bounded question with linked evidence, preserved the same source/method/limitation chain, and did not become the primary path. Do not add unsupported accuracy, adoption, forecast, or impact claims because a winner’s page reports them. Mark any team-reported metric as such, and require a source, benchmark, time window, and denominator before putting it in Bilad’s UI.

Do not add a generic collection of KPI cards, a global globe, unrelated agricultural/public-health/orbital logic, or a second mapping framework. Do not turn breakYear into a causal “event” label: if surfaced, call it a change-point clue or integrity screen and explain that it is not attribution. Keep data-derived marks distinct from atmospheric decoration and use Astryx components/tokens already required by [AGENTS.md](/Users/michaelc/Developer/Bilad/AGENTS.md:13).

## Implementation sequence and verification checklist

1. **Freeze the scientific contract.** Write down the current supported variables, witness mapping, nation set, data window, baseline, units, and limitation language. Treat src/lib/stats.ts, src/lib/analysis.ts, src/lib/evidence.ts, and the generated data as the contract. Do not change the meaning of an existing verdict while improving its presentation.

2. **Reconcile the release.** Resolve six-versus-seven coverage and regenerate or update the checked-in index, copy, tests, and submission artifact. Make the visible count derive from the same source that tests validate.

3. **Build the verdict strip and witness state model.** Start with the existing Chile and Bangladesh case data. Add tests for corroborated, contradicted, unwitnessed, unsupported, unavailable, and failed/retryable states. Keep the full EvidencePanel available below the summary.

4. **Rehearse one three-minute path.** Use the Casebook CTA, a case file, the witness/integrity proof, and an Atlas deep link. Record the path in docs/SUBMISSION.md and use it as the acceptance script.

5. **Add progressive disclosure and the brief/export.** Once the verdict is stable, stage the diagnostics and implement #/brief/<ISO3>, CSV/JSON export, copy-link, and evidence receipt. Every exported number must be traceable to the same browser analysis and provenance shown in the UI.

6. **Finish accessibility and release proof.** Run automated tests plus a real-browser pass at desktop and narrow widths. Check keyboard focus, reduced motion, loading and failure recovery, witness labels, copied URLs, downloaded files, and every source link. Run pnpm exec astryx doctor --built dist after the production build.

Verification checklist:
