# Bilad hackathon winner gap analysis

## What Bilad already does well

### Takeaway

Bilad is already a credible scientific product rather than a generic dashboard: it turns a national Earth-system atlas into a sequence of claims, tests, independent witnesses, integrity checks, and verdicts. Its strongest differentiator is the question “is it real?”—a useful extension of the 2026 NASA challenge, which asks for variable selection, regional time series, rate of change, and significance.

## What is hard to understand, missing, or overbuilt in the first minute

### Takeaway

The first minute has a strong story but too many simultaneous concepts. A judge can see that Bilad is serious, but must infer the primary workflow from “independent witness,” “MERRA-2,” “standard significance test,” “FDR,” “BIC,” map layers, a long evidence panel, and a separate method page. The main gaps are a one-screen verdict summary, explicit coverage/independence labeling, a compact decision artifact, and release consistency.

### Gaps
